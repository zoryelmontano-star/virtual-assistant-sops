HOW I BUILD SYSTEMS

Documentation should help people make better decisions.

01

Start with the trigger

Every workflow begins with a clear event, request, or condition so people know exactly when the SOP applies.

02

Make ownership visible

Responsibility, escalation paths, handoffs, and deadlines are explicit instead of living in someone’s head.

03

Separate steps from judgment

Routine actions become checklists. Decisions become clear rules, thresholds, and exception paths.

04

Close the loop

Every process ends with QA, documentation, follow-up, measurable outcomes, and a clear next state.
